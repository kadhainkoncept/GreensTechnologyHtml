        Description

        AI agents are changing how work gets done. Unlike a simple chatbot that only answers questions, an AI agent can plan, use tools, remember context and take actions to reach a goal. This course gives you a clear, practical understanding of how these systems work and how to put them to use in your business and career.

        We start with the fundamentals: what an AI agent is, what makes AI "agentic", and the essential ingredients every agent needs — a language model, tools, memory and planning. You will then explore the different types of AI agents, from simple reflex agents to complex multi-agent systems.

        From concepts to real implementation

        Next, you will learn how to guide and teach AI agents, and why architecture matters. We cover the most important AI agent architecture patterns and help you choose the right one for your use case.

        Then we get practical. You will follow a framework for implementing AI agents and build a working agentic automation with n8n, step by step. We also look at the infrastructure behind AI agents — hosting, monitoring, cost and security.

        AI agents in business

        Finally, we focus on business value: how to build the business case for AI agents, and how to lead their adoption inside your company.

        By the end of the course, you will be able to:

        • Understand the business value of AI agents
        • Build AI agent systems that meet business goals
        • Lead the adoption of AI agents in your company
